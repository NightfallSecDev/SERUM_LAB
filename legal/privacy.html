Privacy Policy & Data Protocol

01. Information Acquisition Architecture

SERUM LAB collects diagnostic skin profile data, contact coordinates, and transaction ledgers to facilitate precise formulation matching and secure order fulfillment. All diagnostic inputs are processed locally or via encrypted transmission protocols.

02. Encrypted Storage & Efficacy

Your payment credentials and personal medical/skincare history are safeguarded utilizing AES-256 bit encryption standards. We do not monetize, lease, or disseminate patient or customer data matrices to unauthorized third-party conglomerates.

03. Cookie & Diagnostic Tracking

We employ functional telemetry and local storage mechanisms to preserve your cart state and active quiz diagnostic filters across browsing sessions.

04. Protocol Modifications

SERUM LAB reserves the right to amend this privacy architecture as clinical and legal compliance mandates evolve. Inquiries regarding data governance may be directed to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@serumlab.com.
