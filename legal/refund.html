Refund & Replacement Protocol

01. 30-Day Clinical Guarantee

We stand behind the stability and efficacy of our high-concentration active ingredients. If a formulation fails to meet your compatibility thresholds within 30 days of receipt, you may initiate a return protocol for a full refund or diagnostic exchange.

02. Damaged or Compromised Actives

Because active ingredients like L-Ascorbic Acid and Retinol require pristine UV-shielded amber packaging, any transit-induced damage or seal compromise must be reported within 48 hours of delivery to qualify for an immediate express replacement.

03. Return Transit Authorization

To initiate a return, contact our laboratory dispatch at returns@serumlab.com to obtain an RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) tracking barcode.

04. Refund Processing Windows

Approved refunds are credited back to the original encrypted payment gateway within 5-7 business days following laboratory inspection of the returned vessel.
